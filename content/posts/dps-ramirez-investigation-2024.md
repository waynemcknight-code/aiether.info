## DPS Case 2024-0771627: Sexual Assault in Custody

On September 23, 2024, Texas Ranger Cody Rogers opened Public Corruption investigation **2024-0771627** into Colorado County Sheriff's Office Sergeant **Tomas Allen Ramirez** for violations of civil rights involving sexual assault of a person in custody (PC § 39.04(b) | F2).

---

## Investigation Summary

The investigation documents systematic sexual assault by Ramirez against a female inmate held at the Colorado County Jail. The assault pattern includes:

- **08-29-2024**: Initial sexual assault in Ramirez's office at the jail
- **09-22-2024**: Second sexual assault in his office
- **Out-of-custody encounters**: Sexual assaults at Splashway Waterpark and outside custody in Rock Island, Texas
- **Cell phone coordination**: Ramirez used text messages and calls to arrange encounters

### Key Findings

**From victim interview (Supplement 1):**
- Ramirez removed the victim from her cell and took her to his office
- He locked the door, checked deputy locations on his computer
- Ramirez grabbed her breasts and engaged in deviant sexual intercourse
- He ejaculated on her and warned her not to tell anyone
- Pattern repeated on 09-22-2024 with additional details about marijuana use and location tracking
- Out-of-custody encounters documented via cell phone coordination

**From Ramirez interview (Supplement 2):**
- Ramirez denied all sexual contact
- Claimed he knew the victim from "previous law enforcement encounters"
- Acknowledged messaging her via cell phone but denied inappropriate content
- When confronted with victim's account of Splashway encounter, nodded and said "Ok"
- Stated he wanted to talk to her privately about a "cousin who's allegedly selling narcotics"

**Evidence collected (Supplement 4):**
- Marlboro cigarette butts from Ramirez's office desk
- DNA swabs from victim (cheek, throat)
- Victim's jail clothing
- Towels from jail cell #804
- 12-ounce Dr Pepper can from Ramirez's office
- DNA swabs from office door handle
- Cell phone extraction via Cellebrite (Bryan Police Department Crime Scene Unit)

**Cell phone records (Supplement 5):**
- Instant message thread began 08-23-2024
- Victim stated: "let me see that ass so I can take care of something right now"
- Messages discuss deputy locations and whereabouts
- Call log extracted: Victim called Ramirez three times (8/23, 8/23, 8/25)
- Ramirez answered 8/23 calls, did not answer 8/25 call

**Video evidence (Supplement 3):**
- 08-29-2024: Ramirez enters jail 18:47:12, exits with victim 18:48:48, re-enters 19:28:12
- 09-22-2024: Ramirez enters 21:24:37, exits with victim 21:49:06, re-enters 22:57:58

---

## Context: Institutional Pattern

The Ramirez sexual assaults (August-September 2024) occurred in a facility without PREA compliance mechanisms or institutional corrections. This failure to implement safeguards after the Ramirez incidents became evident 16 months later when another inmate was injured by jail staff, demonstrating the lack of corrective action by Colorado County.

The Colorado County Jail operated without PREA (Prison Rape Elimination Act) policy, PREA Coordinator, or PREA training during all documented incidents, despite receiving $4.2M+ in federal State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds (Award ID SLFRP4543) designated for "Jail Rehabilitation."
